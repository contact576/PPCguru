## How a Physiotherapy Clinic Launched Meta Ads at Under $10 a Lead

**Headline results:** 50 Leads / month · $9.95 Cost per lead · 3.03% Click-through rate

PPC Guru launched a Meta Ads lead program for True Life Wellness Physio and improved it fast: in the first two months the clinic went from 32 lead-form enquiries in June 2026 to 50 in July 2026, while cost per lead fell 36% to under $10 — all on a steady budget of about $500 a month.

### About the Client
True Life Wellness Physio is a physiotherapy and wellness clinic serving patients in Ontario, Canada. The practice provides hands-on physiotherapy, injury rehabilitation and related wellness treatments, and depends on a steady flow of new-patient enquiries to fill its schedule. Alongside its search advertising, the clinic added Meta Ads to reach people on Facebook and Instagram and capture new-patient enquiries directly through lead forms.

### How the Account Came to PPC Guru
The clinic partnered with PPC Guru to open a new lead channel on Meta. This was a fresh launch rather than a takeover: PPC Guru built a single focused Instant Form lead campaign, set up the form and tracking, and began optimizing from the first weeks of delivery. The program's first full month was June 2026.

### The Challenge
As a new Meta Ads program on a modest budget, the account had to prove that Facebook and Instagram could produce genuine new-patient enquiries at a sustainable cost before any scaling. The goal for the first two months was simple: grow lead volume while bringing cost per lead down.

### What We Analyzed & Planned
With a modest starting budget, the plan was to prove the channel before scaling it. A single Instant Form campaign kept spend concentrated and learning fast, so PPC Guru could optimize audiences and creative against a clear signal — genuine form submissions — and drive cost per lead down before adding budget.

- Launch one focused Instant Form lead campaign to capture new-patient enquiries in-platform.
- Optimize toward genuine form submissions, not clicks or reach.
- Refine audience and creative weekly on a controlled budget before scaling.
- Track cost per lead month over month as the core success metric.

- **Jul 2026:** 50 lead-form enquiries at $9.95 each — leads up 56% and cost per lead down 36% on a steady budget.

### Lessons for Clinic Owners
1. Start a new Meta channel with one focused lead campaign so the account learns fast on a modest budget.
2. Optimize toward genuine form submissions, not clicks, so spend chases real enquiries.
3. Prove cost per lead before scaling — a lower cost per lead on a steady budget is the signal to invest more.
4. Refine audience and creative every week in the early going; small changes move cost per lead quickly.
5. Judge a launch on its trend, and give it a longer run before drawing firm conclusions.

### Why It Worked
- A single focused campaign concentrated a modest budget and sped up learning.
- Optimizing to form submissions kept the account chasing genuine enquiries.
- Weekly creative and audience refinement drove cost per lead down 36% in one month.
- Holding budget steady meant the gains came from efficiency, not extra spend.
