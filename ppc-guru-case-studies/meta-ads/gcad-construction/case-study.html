About the Client

GCAD Construction is a construction and basement-renovation company serving homeowners in Canada. Its growth depends on a steady flow of project enquiries from homeowners planning basement finishing and renovation work. Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) is being used to reach those homeowners and open a direct conversation at the point of interest, so the number and cost of genuine conversations is the measure that matters for the account.

How the Account Came to PPC Guru

GCAD Construction partnered with PPC Guru to build a paid-social presence from scratch. The account was launched in July 2026 around a messaging objective — so interested homeowners can start a direct conversation — alongside a smaller instant-form campaign. This case study reports the first complete month as an early launch snapshot.

The Challenge

GCAD Construction was starting from zero on paid social, with no existing Meta Ads history, audience data or proven creative. The immediate task was to establish reach among the right homeowners and open genuine project conversations at a workable cost while the account gathered its first data.

What We Analyzed & Planned

PPC Guru launched the account with two Basement-focused messaging campaigns to generate direct conversations and a smaller instant-form campaign to test lead capture. The first priority was to establish reach and a workable cost per conversation before scaling or drawing conclusions.

  • Launch messaging campaigns so high-intent homeowners start a direct conversation about their project.
  • Test an instant-form campaign alongside messaging to compare lead-capture methods.
  • Establish reach and a baseline cost per conversation in month one.
  • Gather at least two to three months of data before measuring growth or optimizing hard.

Results

Why It Worked

  • A messaging objective opened direct conversations with high-intent homeowners from day one.
  • The launch reached about 30,600 homeowners, establishing a base to optimize from.
  • Running messaging and instant-form campaigns together created an early read on what converts.
  • Focusing month one on reach and baseline cost set up disciplined scaling later.

Lessons for Construction & Renovation Firms

  1. Open a direct conversation with homeowners rather than relying on passive form fills.
  2. Expect a higher cost per conversation in the first month while an account learns.
  3. Test messaging and instant-form capture side by side to see which fits your offer.
  4. Give a new account two to three months before judging performance or scaling hard.
  5. Measure genuine conversations, not clicks or reach alone.
