## How PPC Guru Scaled Its Own Meta Ads Lead Engine to 80 Leads a Month at ~$19 Each

**Headline results:** 80 Form leads / month · ~$19 Cost per lead · ▼ 76% cost per lead vs the 2024 pilot

PPC Guru applied its own methodology to its own Meta Ads account — turning a small 2024 pilot that produced about 8 instant-form leads a month at roughly $79 each into a scaled 2026 engine delivering 80 genuine form leads in July 2026 at about $19 each, a cost per lead roughly 76% lower even as monthly budget more than doubled.

### About the Client
PPC Guru is a Canadian digital marketing agency that runs paid search and paid social for clients across a range of local-service industries. This case study covers the agency's own Meta Ads account — the lead engine it uses to generate new-client enquiries. Because it is PPC Guru's own account, it doubles as a live test bed for the instant-form lead-generation approach the agency deploys for clients, which makes the cost and volume of genuine form leads the metric that matters.

### How the Account Came to PPC Guru
The account began as a small pilot in mid-2024 to validate a Meta instant-form approach for agency lead generation. After the pilot proved the concept, the account was paused while attention went to client work, then relaunched and scaled in 2026 with a refined campaign structure and a clear instant-form lead objective. This case study compares the original pilot with the scaled 2026 engine.

### The Challenge
As with many agencies, PPC Guru's own marketing competes for attention with client work, and the early 2024 pilot — while it proved the concept — carried a high cost per lead of around $79 and only a handful of leads a month. The challenge on relaunch was to rebuild the account into a dependable, low-cost lead engine that could scale without the cost per lead climbing.

### What We Analyzed & Planned
PPC Guru treated its own account the way it treats a client's: prove the lead mechanism at small budget, then scale only once the cost per genuine lead is low and stable. The 2026 relaunch concentrated on instant-form campaigns so every dollar could be measured against a real, countable lead rather than a surface engagement.

- Use Meta instant forms so leads are genuine, countable submissions rather than clicks or reach.
- Prove the mechanism at pilot budget before committing to scale.
- Concentrate the 2026 relaunch on lead-objective campaigns and manage to cost per lead.
- Scale monthly budget only after cost per lead proved low and repeatable.

### The Account Timeline
- **2024 pilot (Jun–Sep):** A small validation run averaged a handful of instant-form leads a month; July 2024 delivered 8 leads at about $79 each.
- **Late 2024 – early 2026:** Account paused while focus shifted to client work — no delivery for about 16 months.
- **Mar–Apr 2026:** Relaunch — messaging and lead campaigns rebuilt and audiences re-established at low CPCs.
- **May–Jul 2026:** Instant-form volume scaled to 54, 54 and 80 leads a month at roughly $19–24 each.

### Pilot vs Scale-Up
The 2024 pilot proved the instant-form mechanism at a modest budget, delivering 8 leads in July 2024 at about $79 each. After the 2026 relaunch, instant-form volume scaled sharply while cost per lead fell to roughly a quarter of the pilot level.

| Period | Spend | Form Leads | Cost / Lead |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pilot (Jul 2024) | $633 | 8 | $79.09 |
| Scale-up (May 2026) | $1,083 | 54 | $20.06 |
| Scale-up (Jun 2026) | $1,283 | 54 | $23.75 |
| Now (Jul 2026) | $1,487 | 80 | $18.59 |

### Gradual Improvement, Month by Month
The two eras tell a clear pilot-to-scale story. In 2024 the account produced a handful of instant-form leads a month at a high cost per lead, enough to prove the approach worked. After a pause and a 2026 relaunch — which re-established audiences through lower-cost messaging and lead campaigns in March and April — instant-form volume scaled to 54 leads in May, held at 54 in June, and reached 80 in July at about $19 each. ### Lessons for Agencies & Service Businesses
1. Use instant forms so you count genuine leads, not clicks or reach.
2. Prove the lead mechanism at a small budget before you scale spend.
3. Judge the account on cost per genuine lead, not on impressions or engagement.
4. Scale budget only after cost per lead is low and repeatable across months.
5. Practise on your own account what you deploy for clients — it keeps the method honest.

### Why It Worked
- Instant forms produced genuine, countable leads that spend could be measured against.
- A pilot-first approach de-risked the scale-up and kept cost per lead in check.
- The 2026 rebuild re-established audiences at low CPCs before pushing volume.
- Managing to cost per lead meant more than double the budget delivered ten times the leads.
