## A Custom Cabinetry Brand's Meta Ads Launch: 118 Leads in Month One

**Headline results:** 118 Leads · $5.29 Cost per lead · 2.22% Click-through rate (first month, Jul 2026)

PPC Guru launched a Meta Ads lead program for Lavish Artigiano, a custom cabinetry brand. This is an early-stage snapshot: with only one month of data live, it shows a promising start rather than an established trend.

### About the Client
Lavish Artigiano is a custom cabinetry and artisan millwork brand serving homeowners and renovators in Canada. Cabinetry is a considered, higher-value purchase, so the business relies on a steady stream of qualified enquiries from people planning kitchens, storage and built-in projects. Meta Ads lets the brand showcase its craftsmanship on Facebook and Instagram and capture interested homeowners' details directly through lead forms.

### How the Account Came to PPC Guru
Lavish Artigiano partnered with PPC Guru to build a Meta Ads lead channel from scratch. This is a brand-new launch: PPC Guru set up a single Instant Form lead-and-calls campaign, the lead form and tracking, and began delivery in July 2026. Because only one complete month of data exists so far, this write-up is an honest first-month snapshot rather than a before-and-after case study.

### The Challenge
As a brand-new Meta Ads program, the account first has to prove that Facebook and Instagram can produce genuine, good-value enquiries for a considered purchase like custom cabinetry — before any budget is scaled. The first month's job was simply to validate cost per lead and lead volume on a modest spend.

### What We Analyzed & Planned
The launch plan was to prove the channel efficiently before scaling. A single lead campaign kept the modest budget concentrated so the account could learn quickly against a clear signal — genuine form submissions — while showcasing the brand's cabinetry work to homeowners actively planning projects.

- Optimize toward genuine form submissions rather than clicks or reach.
- Keep the first month's budget modest while validating cost per lead.

### Lessons for Cabinetry & Renovation Businesses
1. Launch a new Meta channel with one focused lead campaign so a modest budget learns fast.
2. Optimize toward genuine form submissions, not clicks, so spend chases real project enquiries.
3. Lead with craft-forward creative — for considered purchases, showing the work builds trust quickly.
4. Validate cost per lead on a modest budget before scaling.
5. Give a launch a few complete months before judging it — one month is a start, not a trend.

### Why It Worked
- A single focused campaign concentrated a modest budget and sped up early learning.
- Optimizing to form submissions kept the account chasing genuine enquiries.
- Craft-forward creative resonated with homeowners planning cabinetry projects.
- A controlled launch budget kept the first month low-risk while cost per lead was validated.
