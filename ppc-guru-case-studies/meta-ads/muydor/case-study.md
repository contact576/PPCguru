## How a Wellness Coach Launched Meta Ads and Landed 112 Leads in Month One

**Headline results:** 112 Leads in month one · ~$4.07 Cost per lead · 5.27% Link CTR

PPC Guru launched a dedicated Instant-Form lead campaign on Meta for a Canadian wellness coach and, in its first full month (July 2026), generated 112 genuine enquiries at about CA$4.07 each. In the months before, the account had been spending on engagement and page-like campaigns that produced almost no real leads.

### About the Client
Living Well with Muydor is a Canadian health and wellness coaching brand that helps people build healthier daily habits and lifestyles. Growth for a personal coaching brand depends on a steady flow of genuine enquiries from people who want to start a program, so the ability to turn ad spend into real contact details matters far more than surface-level engagement such as likes or reactions. Meta (Facebook and Instagram) is the natural channel for reaching that audience where they already spend their time.

### How the Account Came to PPC Guru
Before partnering with PPC Guru, the account had been active on Meta for several months, but the campaigns were built around engagement objectives — earning Facebook likes and the occasional message rather than capturing contact details. Spend was buying reach and reactions, yet very few of those interactions turned into a genuine enquiry the coach could follow up with. The brand brought the account to PPC Guru to convert that audience interest into measurable leads.

### The Challenge
The account was already spending on Meta, but it was optimised for the wrong outcome. Engagement campaigns earned plenty of page likes and reach, yet almost none of that translated into a genuine enquiry: February through April tracked zero form leads, and May and June added only four combined. The brand had audience interest but no reliable way to capture and follow up with the people behind it.

### What We Analyzed & Planned
PPC Guru reviewed the account's recent history and found a clear pattern: money was going toward likes and messaging volume, not lead capture. February through April produced hundreds of page likes but no tracked form leads, and May and June added only a small handful. The plan was to keep the audience insight the earlier activity had built, but point the budget at a proper lead objective with an on-platform Instant Form so enquiries could be captured and counted.

- Shift the objective from engagement / page likes to lead generation with a native Instant Form.
- Capture contact details on-platform to remove landing-page friction for a mobile wellness audience.
- Concentrate budget on the lead campaign rather than spreading it across like-focused ads.
- Measure genuine form leads and cost per lead from day one, not likes or reactions.

### The Launch Timeline
- **Feb–Apr 2026:** Engagement era — the account ran page-like campaigns that earned hundreds of Facebook likes but no tracked form leads.
- **May–Jun 2026:** A few genuine leads trickled in (three in May, one in June) at roughly $60–$84 each, but lead capture was not the focus.
- **July 2026:** PPC Guru launched a dedicated Instant-Form lead campaign — 112 genuine leads in the first month at about $4.07 each.

### Why It Worked
- The objective moved from engagement to genuine lead capture, so spend produced enquiries instead of likes.
- A native Meta Instant Form made it easy for a mobile wellness audience to leave contact details.
- Budget was concentrated on the lead campaign rather than page-like ads.
- Performance was measured on real form leads and cost per lead from the first day.

### Lessons for Coaches & Wellness Brands
1. Likes and reach are not leads — optimise campaigns for the contact details you can actually follow up with.
2. A native Instant Form removes friction for a mobile audience and lifts completion rates.
3. Concentrate budget on the objective that produces enquiries rather than spreading it across engagement ads.
4. Measure cost per genuine lead from day one so you know what an enquiry really costs.
5. Audience interest built by earlier content can be redirected into lead capture with the right campaign structure.
