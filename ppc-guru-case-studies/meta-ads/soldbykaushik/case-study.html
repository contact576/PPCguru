About the Client

Sold by Kaushik is a Canadian real estate professional who helps buyers and sellers through the property process. For an individual agent, a dependable flow of enquiries — people ready to talk about buying or selling — is the engine of the business. Meta (Facebook and Instagram) lets an agent reach local prospects and open a direct conversation, whether through a lead form or a WhatsApp chat, at the moment they are considering a move.

How the Account Came to PPC Guru

The agent partnered with PPC Guru to build a measurable lead source on Meta rather than relying on referrals and organic reach alone. PPC Guru launched the account in July 2026 with two complementary campaigns: a native Instant-Form campaign to capture contact details, and a WhatsApp messaging campaign to start direct conversations with interested prospects.

The Challenge

As a new launch, the account had no Meta history to build on — no proven audiences, creative or cost benchmarks. The task was to establish reliable lead capture across two objectives and produce genuine enquiries the agent could act on, while keeping a clean read on what each enquiry costs.

What We Analyzed & Planned

Because this is a fresh launch, the priority was to stand up clean lead capture and measurement from day one and to test two ways of reaching prospects side by side — a form for people who prefer to leave details, and WhatsApp for people who prefer to chat. The first month establishes a baseline cost per enquiry that PPC Guru will optimise against as more data accumulates.

  • Launch a native Instant-Form campaign to capture buyer and seller contact details on-platform.
  • Run a parallel WhatsApp messaging campaign to open direct conversations with prospects.
  • Measure cost per form lead and cost per conversation from the first day.
  • Use month-one results as the baseline for ongoing optimisation.

Why It Worked

  • Two lead objectives launched together gave the agent both form leads and live conversations.
  • On-platform capture kept enquiries fast and frictionless for a mobile audience.
  • Clean measurement from day one set an honest cost-per-enquiry baseline.
  • The form campaign delivered leads at about $19.33 each in its first month.

Lessons for Real Estate Agents

  1. Give prospects two ways to reach you — a form for details and WhatsApp for a quick chat.
  2. Capture contact details on-platform so no enquiry slips away to a slow landing page.
  3. Measure cost per genuine enquiry from day one, not clicks or reach.
  4. Treat the first month as a baseline and optimise from real data rather than assumptions.
  5. Follow up with new leads fast — speed to contact matters in real estate.
