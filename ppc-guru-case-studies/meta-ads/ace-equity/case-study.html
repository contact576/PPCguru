About the Client

Ace Equity is a Canadian financial services firm that helps clients with their financial goals. In financial services, trust and timely contact matter: growth depends on reaching people who are actively considering their options and giving them an easy, credible way to start a conversation. Meta (Facebook and Instagram) lets the firm reach that audience and open a direct line — through a lead form or a WhatsApp chat — while interest is high.

How the Account Came to PPC Guru

The firm partnered with PPC Guru to build a measurable enquiry source on Meta. PPC Guru launched the account in July 2026 with two complementary campaigns under a 'Loyalty' theme: a native Instant-Form campaign to capture contact details, and a WhatsApp messaging campaign to start direct conversations with interested prospects.

The Challenge

As a new launch, the account had no Meta history to build on — no proven audiences, creative or cost benchmarks. In a considered category like financial services, the task was to establish credible lead capture across two objectives and produce genuine enquiries the team could act on, while keeping a clean read on what each enquiry costs.

What We Analyzed & Planned

As a fresh launch, the focus was on clean lead capture and measurement from day one, and on testing two contact routes side by side — a form for people who prefer to leave details and WhatsApp for those who prefer to chat. The first month sets a baseline cost per enquiry that PPC Guru will optimise against as more data accumulates.

  • Launch a native Instant-Form campaign to capture client enquiries on-platform.
  • Run a parallel WhatsApp messaging campaign to open direct conversations with prospects.
  • Measure cost per form lead and cost per conversation from the first day.
  • Use month-one results as the baseline for ongoing optimisation.

Why It Worked

  • Two lead objectives launched together gave the firm both form leads and live conversations.
  • On-platform capture kept enquiries fast and frictionless for a mobile audience.
  • Clean measurement from day one set an honest cost-per-enquiry baseline.
  • The launch produced 16 genuine enquiries across form and WhatsApp in the first month.

Lessons for Financial Services Firms

  1. Give prospects two ways to reach you — a form for details and WhatsApp for a quick chat.
  2. Capture contact details on-platform so enquiries are fast and credible for a considered category.
  3. Measure cost per genuine enquiry from day one, not clicks or reach.
  4. Treat the first month as a baseline and optimise from real data rather than assumptions.
  5. Follow up quickly — in financial services, timely, trusted contact wins the conversation.
