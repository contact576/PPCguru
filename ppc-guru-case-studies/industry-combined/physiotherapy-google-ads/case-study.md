## How PPC Guru Grows Physiotherapy Clinics with Google Ads

**Headline results:** 6 Clinics managed · ~105 Combined leads / month · ~$55 Avg. cost per lead

PPC Guru manages Google Ads for a portfolio of physiotherapy and rehabilitation clinics across Canada. Using one repeatable playbook — genuine call and booking tracking, high-intent search targeting, and disciplined optimization — the clinics together generate roughly 105 tracked patient enquiries a month in the most recent quarter (Apr–Jun 2026), at an average of about $55 per lead.

### About the Client
This case study covers a group of physiotherapy, rehabilitation and sports-injury clinics that PPC Guru manages on Google Ads. They range from long-established practices to clinics launched in 2026, and most schedule patients through booking systems such as JaneApp. For every one of them, growth depends on a steady flow of new-patient enquiries — mainly phone calls and online bookings from people searching for treatment nearby — which makes reliable measurement of calls and bookings the foundation of the account.

### How the Account Came to PPC Guru
The clinics came to PPC Guru in two situations. Some were new to Google Ads or had recently launched and needed a lead-generation account built from scratch. Others had an existing presence — steady traffic, but conversion tracking that captured page views and map actions rather than genuine phone-and-booking enquiries. In both cases the opportunity was the same: build a clean, measurable Search account focused on patients ready to book.

### What We Analyzed & Planned
PPC Guru applies the same audit to each clinic: confirm what is actually being tracked, map the high-intent treatment searches, and identify where budget is going to informational or low-value clicks. The shared plan is measurement first, then concentrate spend on the searches that produce booked appointments, then scale only where the booking data proves it.

- Rebuild conversion tracking to capture phone calls, form submissions and completed bookings — importing bookings as offline conversions where the clinic's system supports it.
- Separate treatment-intent searches from informational queries and general wellness terms.
- Consolidate into focused Search campaigns so budget and conversion data are concentrated.
- Scale budget per clinic only once genuine lead volume and cost per lead are proven.

### Why Booking-System Tracking Matters for Clinics
Several of these clinics schedule patients through JaneApp or similar systems. Where the system supports it, PPC Guru imports completed bookings back into Google Ads as offline conversions, so automated bidding optimizes toward patients who actually book — not toward whoever clicks a 'Book' button or a Google Business Profile map pin. That closed loop is the single biggest reason the clinics generate genuine enquiries efficiently instead of paying for low-value actions.

### Lessons for Physiotherapy Clinic Owners
1. Track phone calls and completed appointments — not button clicks, map views or page visits.
2. Separate treatment-intent searches from informational and general-wellness queries.
3. Let a new account gather enough conversions before trusting automated bidding.
4. Scale budget only after genuine lead volume and cost per lead are proven.
5. Review the actual search terms continuously and keep the negative-keyword list current.
