About the Client

EcoCare Home Comfort is a Canadian HVAC and home-comfort company. Its customers fall into two groups: homeowners with an urgent need (a failing furnace or AC) who search Google and want to call now, and a much larger group who can be reached earlier on Facebook and Instagram with a maintenance or install offer. PPC Guru runs both channels so EcoCare covers the whole funnel.

How the Account Came to PPC Guru

EcoCare launched on Meta with PPC Guru in August 2025 and added a managed Google Ads account in late 2025. Rather than treat them separately, PPC Guru runs them as one HVAC growth programme: Google for bottom-of-funnel intent, Meta for volume and demand generation — each measured on genuine leads, not clicks.

The Challenge

What We Analyzed & Planned

The plan was to match each channel to what it does best. On Google, track calls as the primary conversion and target urgent service searches. On Meta, run instant lead forms with a clear offer and scale reach as cost per lead held up. Keep both measured on genuine enquiries so budget can move to whichever channel is delivering.

  • Google: track phone calls as the primary conversion; target urgent HVAC service searches.
  • Meta: run instant lead forms with a clear seasonal offer; scale reach on proven cost per lead.
  • Measure both channels on genuine leads (calls / forms), excluding vanity actions.
  • Shift budget toward whichever channel delivers the best cost per genuine lead.

Why HVAC Needs Both Channels

A homeowner whose furnace just failed searches Google and calls immediately — that's high-intent, and Google captures it. But most homeowners aren't in crisis today; they can be reached earlier on Facebook and Instagram with a tune-up or install offer, building a pipeline for the weeks ahead. Running both, measured on genuine leads, means EcoCare captures urgent demand and creates new demand — and PPC Guru can move budget to whichever channel is converting best at any point in the season.

Lessons for HVAC Business Owners

  1. Treat Google and Meta as one programme — intent capture plus demand generation.
  2. On Google, track phone calls as the primary conversion; HVAC is phone-led.
  3. On Meta, use instant lead forms with a clear seasonal offer to build volume.
  4. Measure both channels on genuine leads so you can move budget to the best performer.
  5. Expect a higher cost per lead on Google calls — they are worth more per job.

Where They Stand Today

EcoCare Home Comfort now runs a full-funnel HVAC programme: about 14 high-intent phone leads a month from Google Search and roughly 396 lead-form enquiries a month from Meta in the most recent quarter, each measured on genuine enquiries. The company captures urgent service demand and generates new demand at the same time — with PPC Guru able to shift budget to whichever channel delivers the best cost per lead as the season changes.
