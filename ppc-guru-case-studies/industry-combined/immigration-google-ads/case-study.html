The Challenge

What We Analyzed & Planned

PPC Guru's plan for each firm was the same: track calls and form submissions cleanly, target the specific immigration services the firm offers, filter out unrelated 'how to' and news searches, and scale budget only where the enquiry cost held up.

  • Set up call and lead-form tracking, excluding pop-up and page-view actions that inflate raw conversion counts.
  • Target specific, high-intent service searches and separate them from informational queries.
  • Concentrate budget on the services and locations that produce genuine enquiries.
  • Scale spend per firm only after cost per enquiry proved sustainable.

Why Call Tracking Is Critical for Immigration Firms

Immigration enquiries are overwhelmingly phone calls — a prospective client wants to speak to a consultant now. If an account only counts form fills or button clicks, it optimizes toward the wrong thing. PPC Guru tracks calls as the primary conversion for these firms and excludes pop-up and page-view actions, so bidding and budget chase genuine consultations.

Lessons for Immigration Consultants

  1. Track phone calls as the primary enquiry — they are the dominant lead type in immigration.
  2. Exclude pop-up, page-view and 'arrange consultation' button clicks from your conversion counts.
  3. Target specific services (study permit, PR, sponsorship) rather than broad 'immigration' terms.
  4. Add negatives aggressively to filter news, DIY and unrelated searches.
  5. Scale budget only once cost per genuine enquiry is proven.
