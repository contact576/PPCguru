About the Client

This study covers construction and home-renovation companies that PPC Guru manages on Meta. Meta's reach and Messenger flow make it a strong fit for turning that interest into direct enquiries.

The Challenge

What We Analyzed & Planned

  • Target local homeowners in each company's service radius.

  • Optimize toward started conversations and refine audiences/creative continuously.

Why It Worked

  • Messaging objective that matches how homeowners actually enquire.
  • Local targeting focused on each company's service area.
  • Real project creative that builds trust quickly.
  • Optimization toward started conversations, not vanity reach.

Why Messaging Beats Forms for Renovation Leads

Homeowners planning a renovation want a conversation, not a form. A messaging objective lets them ask a question straight from the ad, which lowers the barrier to enquire and gives the company a warm thread to follow up on. For local trades, that direct conversation is usually a higher-quality first touch than a cold form fill — which is why both companies optimize toward started conversations.

Lessons for Construction & Renovation Owners

  1. Make it easy to start a conversation — messaging lowers the barrier for renovation enquiries.
  2. Lead with real photos of your work; homeowners buy trust before they buy a build.
  3. Target your true service radius so budget isn't wasted on out-of-area reach.
  4. Give a new account a few weeks of data before judging cost per conversation.
  5. Follow up on conversations fast — speed to reply drives booked site visits.
