About the Client

Bindra World Immigration Terminal is an immigration consultancy based in Brampton, Ontario, serving clients across the province on matters such as work permits and post-graduation work permits (PGWP). Prospective clients typically search for a local, trustworthy consultant and then reach out by phone, contact form or WhatsApp. Because those enquiries are the start of a high-value consulting relationship, capturing and measuring genuine phone and form leads is central to the account's success.

How the Account Came to PPC Guru

The consultancy came to PPC Guru to build a measurable Google Ads presence and reach people actively searching for immigration help across Ontario. PPC Guru launched a focused Search campaign in early 2026, and once phone-call tracking was firing, the account's genuine lead volume scaled quickly.

The Challenge

For an immigration consultancy, the enquiries that matter are phone calls and form submissions from people ready to talk about their case. Early in the launch the account was capturing WhatsApp button clicks but not yet recording phone calls, so the genuine lead picture only became clear once call tracking was firing. The task was to establish that tracking, then scale spend on the searches that produced real enquiries — not just clicks or button taps.

What We Analyzed & Planned

The plan was to capture high-intent immigration searches on Search, wire up genuine lead tracking (calls and forms), and then scale budget as the real enquiry data proved out — keeping WhatsApp button clicks and Google Business Profile local actions separate from the true lead count.

  • Focus a Search campaign on high-intent queries like 'immigration consultant near me' and work-permit searches.
  • Establish genuine phone-call and form tracking so reporting reflects real client enquiries.
  • Separate WhatsApp button clicks and local actions from the genuine lead count.
  • Scale budget as tracked phone and form leads grew and cost per lead fell.

Gradual Improvement, Month by Month

Once phone-call tracking went live in May, genuine leads scaled quickly: 6 in May, 44 in June and about 56 in July. Crucially, this was not just more spend buying more clicks — cost per lead roughly halved over the same window, from about $35 to $18, and the conversion rate rose from around 11% to 16%. The added budget brought proportionally more genuine enquiries, not just more traffic.

Why Call Tracking Changed the Picture

For the first weeks after launch, the account could see WhatsApp button clicks but not phone calls — so the true number of client enquiries was understated. Once website-call and call-from-ads tracking was firing in May 2026, the account could finally optimize toward real phone enquiries rather than button taps. That is what let Maximize Conversions bidding find more of the searches that turn into genuine immigration consultations, and it is why lead volume climbed while cost per lead fell.

Lessons for Immigration Consultants

  1. Track phone calls and form submissions — for a consultancy, those are the enquiries worth measuring.
  2. Keep WhatsApp button clicks and Google Business Profile local actions separate from your genuine lead count.
  3. Get call tracking firing early — until it is, your reported lead numbers understate reality.
  4. Scale budget on proven, tracked leads and a falling cost per lead, not on raw clicks.
  5. Keep negative keywords current so budget reaches genuine immigration-intent searches.

Within months of launch, Bindra World Immigration Terminal scaled from 6 tracked phone and form leads in its first fully-tracked month to about 56 a month by July 2026 — while cost per lead roughly halved to $18 and the conversion rate climbed to around 16%. The account now has clean genuine-lead tracking, a focused Search structure and a demonstrated, efficient path to scale as demand for immigration services continues.
