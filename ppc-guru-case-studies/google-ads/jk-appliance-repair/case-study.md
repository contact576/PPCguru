## How an Appliance Repair Company Reached ~40 Google Ads Leads a Month in One Quarter

**Headline results:** ~40 Leads / month · ~$34 Cost per lead · ~21% Conversion rate

PPC Guru launched a focused Google Ads account for JK Appliance Repair with call and form tracking from day one. Within its first full quarter under management (Apr–Jun 2026) the account was generating about 40 genuine phone and form leads a month at roughly $34 per lead, with a conversion rate above 20% on high-intent repair searches.

### About the Client
JK Appliance Repair Inc. is an appliance repair company serving Toronto and the surrounding GTA. The business fixes refrigerators, air conditioners, small kitchen appliances, vacuums and similar household units, and depends on a steady flow of urgent, local service calls. Most of that demand shows up on Google at the exact moment something breaks, which makes search advertising a natural fit and reliable call tracking essential to measuring real jobs.

### How the Account Came to PPC Guru
JK Appliance Repair came to PPC Guru to build a measurable Google Ads presence from the ground up. Rather than run traffic and hope, the goal was a lean Search account aimed only at people actively looking to repair an appliance nearby, with every phone call and form submission tracked so the business could see which searches turned into real service enquiries.

### The Challenge
Building a new Google Ads account means there is no history for the system to learn from and no proof yet of which searches produce paying jobs. For an urgent service like appliance repair, the account also has to capture phone calls reliably from the very first week, because most enquiries are calls rather than form fills. The task was to reach dependable lead volume quickly without letting spend drift onto low-intent or out-of-area searches.

### What We Analyzed & Planned
PPC Guru set the account up around measurement first. Because appliance repair is an urgent, phone-driven service, the priority was capturing calls (from both ads and the website) alongside contact-form submissions, then concentrating budget on the highest-intent repair searches so automated bidding had clean signals to optimize toward.

- Stand up call tracking for both ad call extensions and website calls, plus a contact-form conversion.
- Target high-intent repair queries by appliance and by neighbourhood across Toronto and the GTA.
- Run a single focused Search campaign so budget and conversion data stay concentrated.
- Use Maximize Conversions bidding once the tracking was live and gathering data.

### The Launch Timeline
- **January 2026:** Account launch and setup — tracking wired up and the first budget went live while the campaign ramped.
- **February 2026:** First full month of delivery — 22 tracked phone and form leads at about $50 each.
- **April 2026:** Campaign relaunched and scaled — 43 tracked leads in the month at about $34 each.
- **Q2 2026 (Apr–Jun):** About 40 genuine leads a month at roughly $34 per lead and a conversion rate above 20%.

### Lessons for Home-Service Owners
1. Track phone calls from day one — for repair services, calls are the majority of real enquiries.
2. Start focused: one tight Search campaign on high-intent searches beats spreading budget across everything.
3. Target by appliance and by area so budget reaches people ready to book a nearby repair.
4. Give a new account a few weeks to gather data before judging cost per lead.
5. Scale budget only once the lead volume and cost per lead are proven, not before.

### Why It Worked
- Call and form tracking from launch meant bidding optimized toward real enquiries, not clicks.
- Budget stayed concentrated on high-intent repair searches across the GTA.
- A single focused Search campaign gathered conversion data quickly and cleanly.
- Cost per lead settled around $34 as the account matured, keeping growth efficient.
