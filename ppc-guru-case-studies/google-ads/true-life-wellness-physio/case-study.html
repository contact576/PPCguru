03The Opportunity

True Life Wellness Physio already had a multi-year Google Ads presence, so the clinic was reaching searchers and generating clicks. The available account history shows that clicks and impressions were consistent, which pointed to a clear opportunity: convert more of that existing traffic into booked appointments. Under the earlier structure the conversion rate sat below 1%, suggesting room to focus spend on higher-intent physiotherapy searches, tighten keyword and match-type coverage, and strengthen conversion tracking so that calls and booking requests were measured reliably. In short, the demand was already there — the opportunity was to build a more conversion-focused paid search structure around it and give the clinic clearer visibility into which searches produced real enquiries.

04PPC Guru’s Strategy

PPC Guru rebuilt the account with a lead-generation-first structure and re-established clean conversion measurement.

  • Restructured the account into focused search campaigns targeting high-intent physiotherapy and injury-treatment queries.
  • Rebuilt keyword coverage with tighter match types and an ongoing negative-keyword programme to filter low-intent traffic.
  • Strengthened conversion tracking so booking form submissions and phone calls were measured consistently.
  • Aligned ad copy and extensions with the clinic's core services and local service area.
  • Concentrated budget on the campaigns and search terms that produced booked appointments.
  • Applied ongoing search-term review and bid optimization to compound results month over month.

07Business Impact

For a physiotherapy clinic, the value of paid search is measured in booked appointments, not just clicks. By focusing spend on higher-intent searches and measuring bookings and calls accurately, PPC Guru turned a largely traffic-driven account into a dependable source of appointment requests. The clinic moved from a handful of tracked conversions each month to a steady stream of enquiries at a fraction of the previous cost, giving the practice a predictable local lead pipeline and a clearer picture of which searches drive real patients. That efficiency also created room to scale confidently, because each additional dollar of budget now maps to measurable demand.

08Why the Campaign Worked

  • Higher-intent keyword and match-type targeting focused spend on searchers ready to book.
  • Stronger conversion tracking made booking requests and calls measurable and optimisable.
  • Continuous search-term and negative-keyword refinement reduced wasted clicks over time.
  • Budget was concentrated on the campaigns and queries that produced appointments.
  • A clean, conversion-first account structure created a foundation for ongoing optimization.

10Conclusion

True Life Wellness Physio shows what a conversion-focused rebuild can do for an established local advertiser. With the same demand and a slightly smaller budget, the clinic moved from fewer than two tracked conversions a month to roughly sixteen, while cost per conversion fell by more than 90%. The transition reflects the compounding value of a clear campaign structure, reliable measurement, and disciplined ongoing optimization — the foundation PPC Guru continues to build on for the account.
