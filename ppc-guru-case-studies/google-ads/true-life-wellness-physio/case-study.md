## 5. The Opportunity
True Life Wellness Physio already had an established Google Ads presence, so the clinic was reaching searchers and earning clicks each month. The available account history shows steady impressions and traffic, but only a small number of tracked conversions — which pointed to a clear opportunity in measurement and structure. With reliable call and booking-form tracking, the clinic could see which searches actually produced enquiries; with a tighter, higher-intent keyword structure, more of that existing traffic could be turned into booked appointments. The demand was already there. The opportunity was to build a conversion-focused paid search account around it and give the practice dependable visibility into the leads its budget was generating.

## 6. PPC Guru's Strategy
PPC Guru rebuilt the account with a lead-generation-first structure and re-established clean conversion measurement.

- Restructured the account into focused search campaigns targeting high-intent physiotherapy and injury-treatment queries.
- Strengthened conversion tracking so booking-form submissions and phone calls were measured consistently.
- Rebuilt keyword coverage with tighter match types and an ongoing negative-keyword programme to filter low-intent traffic.
- Aligned ad copy and extensions with the clinic's core services and local service area.
- Concentrated budget on the campaigns and search terms that produced booked appointments.
- Applied ongoing search-term review and bid adjustments to keep cost per booking stable as volume grew.
