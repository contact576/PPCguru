## 5. The Opportunity
True Life Wellness Physio already had a multi-year Google Ads presence, so the clinic was reaching searchers and generating clicks. The available account history shows that clicks and impressions were consistent, which pointed to a clear opportunity: convert more of that existing traffic into booked appointments. Under the earlier structure the conversion rate sat below 1%, suggesting room to focus spend on higher-intent physiotherapy searches, tighten keyword and match-type coverage, and strengthen conversion tracking so that calls and booking requests were measured reliably. In short, the demand was already there — the opportunity was to build a more conversion-focused paid search structure around it and give the clinic clearer visibility into which searches produced real enquiries.

## 6. PPC Guru's Strategy
PPC Guru rebuilt the account with a lead-generation-first structure and re-established clean conversion measurement.
- Restructured the account into focused search campaigns targeting high-intent physiotherapy and injury-treatment queries.
- Rebuilt keyword coverage with tighter match types and an ongoing negative-keyword programme to filter low-intent traffic.
- Strengthened conversion tracking so booking form submissions and phone calls were measured consistently.
- Aligned ad copy and extensions with the clinic's core services and local service area.
- Concentrated budget on the campaigns and search terms that produced booked appointments.
- Applied ongoing search-term review and bid optimization to compound results month over month.

For a physiotherapy clinic, the value of paid search is measured in booked appointments, not just clicks. By focusing spend on higher-intent searches and measuring bookings and calls accurately, PPC Guru turned a largely traffic-driven account into a dependable source of appointment requests.
