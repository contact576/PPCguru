## How a North York Physiotherapy Clinic Cut Cost per Call from $144 to $35

**Headline results:** ~12 Call leads / month · ~$35 Cost per lead · ~14% Conversion rate

PPC Guru launched a Google Ads account for a North York physiotherapy and sports-injury clinic with call tracking from the start. Across its first complete quarter (Apr–Jun 2026) the account grew to about 12 tracked phone-call leads a month at roughly $35 each — down from about $144 per call in the launch month — as budget concentrated on high-intent local physiotherapy searches.

### About the Client
NorthYork Healthcare Associates is a physiotherapy and sports-injury clinic based in North York, Toronto, offering hands-on physiotherapy and rehabilitation with online booking through JaneApp. New-patient growth depends on reaching people searching for physiotherapy nearby and getting them on the phone, so reliable call tracking is central to the account.

### How the Account Came to PPC Guru
The clinic came to PPC Guru to build a measurable Google Ads presence focused on new-patient calls. Rather than chase clicks, the goal was a lean Search account aimed at high-intent local physiotherapy searches, with every phone call tracked so the clinic could see cost per genuine enquiry and scale from there.

### The Challenge
A brand-new account has no history for the system to learn from, and early cost per lead is almost always high while it gathers data — the launch month came in around $144 per call. For a physiotherapy clinic, the account also has to capture phone calls reliably from week one, because that is how most patients book. The task was to bring cost per call down quickly while growing call volume, without letting spend drift onto low-intent searches.

### What We Analyzed & Planned
Because physiotherapy patients overwhelmingly reach out by phone, PPC Guru built the account around call tracking first, then focused budget on local, treatment-intent searches. The plan was to launch lean, let the account gather clean conversion data, and bring cost per call down as Maximize Conversions bidding learned.

- Track calls from both ads and the website as the primary lead signal.
- Focus budget on high-intent local searches like 'physiotherapy near me' and 'North York physiotherapy clinic'.
- Run a single focused Search campaign so the account gathers conversion data quickly.
- Use Maximize Conversions bidding and let cost per call settle as data builds.

### The Launch Timeline
- **February 2026:** Account launch — call tracking wired up; first month produced 2 tracked calls at about $144 each while the account gathered data.
- **March 2026:** 4 tracked calls as the campaign warmed up and cost per call started falling.
- **April 2026:** 9 tracked calls at about $38 each — the account found efficient footing.
- **Q2 2026 (Apr–Jun):** About 12 tracked calls a month, peaking at 20 in June, at roughly $35 per call and a conversion rate near 14%.

### Lessons for Clinic Owners
1. Track phone calls from day one — for a physiotherapy clinic, calls are how most patients book.
2. Expect a high cost per lead in the first weeks and give the account time to gather data before judging it.
3. Keep budget on high-intent local searches so spend reaches people ready to book treatment nearby.
4. Let Maximize Conversions bidding learn on clean call data to bring cost per call down.
5. Have booking tracking ready so measurement can extend from call to booked appointment as volume grows.

### Why It Worked
- Call tracking from launch meant bidding optimized toward real phone enquiries, not clicks.
- A single focused Search campaign gathered clean conversion data quickly.
- Budget stayed on high-intent local physiotherapy searches.
- Cost per call fell from about $144 to roughly $35 as the account matured.
