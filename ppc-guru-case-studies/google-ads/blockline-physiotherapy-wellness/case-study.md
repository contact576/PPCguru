## Launching a Physiotherapy Clinic to ~13 Google Ads Leads a Month

**Headline results:** ~13 Genuine leads / month · ~$56 Cost per lead · ~13% Conversion rate

PPC Guru launched Blockline Physiotherapy & Wellness on Google Ads with call and lead-form tracking in place, and the clinic's first full months produced about 13 genuine phone and form leads a month at roughly $56 each — with genuine enquiries counted separately from Google Business Profile map actions and booking-button clicks.

### About the Client
Blockline Physiotherapy & Wellness is a physiotherapy and wellness clinic serving patients in the Kitchener–Waterloo area of Ontario, Canada. The clinic provides hands-on physiotherapy and related wellness care, and depends on a steady flow of new-patient enquiries — phone calls and online contact requests from people searching for treatment nearby. Google Ads is the clinic's channel for reaching those high-intent local searchers, which makes clean measurement of calls and form submissions important from the very first month.

### How the Account Came to PPC Guru
Blockline Physiotherapy & Wellness partnered with PPC Guru in spring 2026 to launch its Google Ads presence. As a fresh build, the priority was a focused local Search campaign paired with proper conversion tracking, so the clinic could see genuine phone and form enquiries rather than vanity map actions.

### The Challenge
As a brand-new Google Ads account, the clinic had no history to build on and had to establish genuine local demand from a standing start. A further challenge was measurement clarity: most of the account's recorded activity is Google Business Profile map actions and booking-button clicks, which can badly overstate results if counted as leads. Isolating true phone and form enquiries was essential to report honestly and to steer bidding correctly.

### What We Analyzed & Planned
PPC Guru built the account around a single local Search campaign for Kitchener and set up call tracking (ads and website), lead-form conversions, and clear separation of genuine leads from Google Business Profile local actions. That separation matters: the raw account activity is dominated by directions, website visits and booking-button clicks, so counting only real calls and forms keeps the numbers honest from day one.

- Launch a single, focused local Search campaign targeting Kitchener–Waterloo.
- Set up call tracking for both ad calls and website calls to the clinic line.
- Add lead-form conversions for online contact requests.
- Separate genuine calls and forms from Google Business Profile local actions and booking-button clicks so reporting reflects real enquiries.

### Lessons for Clinic Owners
1. Count real calls and form submissions — not map directions or booking-button clicks — so early results are honest.
2. Set up call tracking before launch; in a clinic, most enquiries come by phone.
3. Start with one focused local Search campaign to concentrate budget and data.
4. Give a new account a full quarter before drawing firm conclusions.
5. Keep the search terms clean early so budget reaches treatment-intent searches.
