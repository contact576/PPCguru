PPC Guru took a rehabilitation clinic off a minimal-budget Smart Campaign — one whose recorded 'conversions' were mostly Google Maps directions and button taps — and rebuilt it into two focused Search campaigns with genuine call and JaneApp booking tracking. The result is a dependable, measurable stream of around 22 real phone-call leads a month in the most recent quarter (Apr–Jun 2026), at roughly $63 per lead.

About the Client

The client is a rehabilitation clinic in Ontario, Canada offering physiotherapy and massage therapy, with patient scheduling handled through the JaneApp booking and practice-management system. Like most local clinics, its growth depends on a steady flow of new-patient enquiries — mainly phone calls from people searching for treatment nearby. Google Ads is the clinic's primary channel for reaching those high-intent searchers, which makes reliable measurement of calls and bookings central to the account's success.

How the Account Came to PPC Guru

For several years the account ran as a low-budget Google Smart Campaign, spending only around $300 a month. It logged plenty of 'conversions', but on inspection most were Google local actions — map-direction requests and click-to-call taps on the Business Profile — rather than separately tracked phone-call leads or bookings. The clinic had little real visibility into how many genuine enquiries the spend produced. In late 2022 the account was handed to PPC Guru for a full rebuild focused on measurement and controllable Search campaigns.

The Challenge

The account had years of history and a healthy-looking conversion count, but the numbers were misleading. Running as a low-budget Smart Campaign, it recorded mostly Google Maps directions and click-to-call taps — local actions, not measurable enquiries. There was no reliable line of sight from ad spend to a genuine phone call or a booked appointment, which made it impossible to know what was actually working or to scale with confidence.

What We Analyzed & Planned

Before scaling anything, PPC Guru audited the account's long history, conversion tracking and campaign structure. The review showed genuine local demand but a measurement gap: the Smart Campaign optimized toward whatever was easiest to trigger — directions and profile taps — instead of real calls and bookings. The plan put honest tracking first, moved the account onto dedicated Search campaigns, and only then scaled budget against measured phone-call leads.

  • Rebuild conversion tracking to capture genuine phone calls (from ads and the website) and JaneApp bookings, and separate them from Google local actions.
  • Move off the Smart Campaign onto dedicated Search campaigns for physiotherapy and massage therapy, for full control of keywords and budget.
  • Use Maximize Conversions bidding once trustworthy call data was flowing.
  • Scale budget deliberately against measured phone-call leads rather than vanity local actions.

Why It Worked

  • Honest call and booking tracking replaced a conversion count inflated by Google local actions.
  • Dedicated Search campaigns gave full control over keywords, budget and negatives.
  • Budget was scaled only after measured phone leads proved the channel worked.
  • Ongoing search-term hygiene kept traffic focused on genuine treatment-intent queries.

Why the JaneApp Booking Integration Matters

JaneApp is the clinic's booking and practice-management system. Feeding genuine phone calls and completed bookings back into Google Ads — instead of counting Maps directions and profile taps — is what turned a vanity conversion count into an honest one. It lets automated bidding chase real patient enquiries, and it gives the clinic a true picture of which searches produce booked treatment. Without that closed loop, Google optimises toward whatever is cheapest to trigger, which is exactly how the account behaved before the rebuild.

Lessons for Clinic Owners

  1. A high conversion count means nothing if most of it is Maps directions and profile taps — track genuine calls and bookings.
  2. Smart Campaigns trade control for convenience; a dedicated Search campaign lets you steer budget toward treatment-intent searches.
  3. Get honest measurement in place before scaling budget, not after.
  4. Feed real bookings back into the account so bidding optimises toward patients, not button clicks.
  5. Expect natural month-to-month variation; judge the account on its steady baseline, not any single month.

Where They Stand Today

Today the clinic runs on a measured, controllable Search program instead of an automated Smart Campaign. In the most recent complete quarter (April–June 2026) it generated about 22 genuine phone-call leads a month at roughly $63 per lead, with call and JaneApp booking tracking giving a true view of demand — up from a pre-rebuild setup that recorded mostly Maps directions and profile taps rather than real enquiries. Because every dollar of spend is now tied to genuine calls and bookings, the clinic can scale with confidence rather than guesswork.
