About the Client

Therapy Villa is a psychotherapy and mental-health practice serving clients across the Greater Toronto Area, with counselling for anxiety, trauma, couples and related concerns and online booking through JaneApp. Demand comes from people actively searching for a therapist nearby, so the account's success depends on capturing genuine enquiries — phone calls, form submissions and booked consultations — rather than surface-level clicks.

How the Account Came to PPC Guru

Before PPC Guru, Therapy Villa's Google Ads had grown into a patchwork of overlapping campaigns — several generic Search campaigns, Performance Max and various one-off tests — with inconsistent conversion tracking that leaned heavily on button clicks and page views. Phone calls, the most valuable enquiry for a therapy practice, were largely not tracked. The practice brought the account to PPC Guru in January 2026 for a clean rebuild focused on measurable leads.

The Challenge

The account was spending on strong therapy demand, but it was impossible to tell how much of it was working. Conversion counts were inflated by outbound clicks on 'book' and phone-number buttons and by page views, while actual phone calls — the way most therapy clients reach out — were not tracked at all. Budget was also split across many overlapping campaigns, so no single campaign gathered enough clean data to optimize well.

What We Analyzed & Planned

PPC Guru's review found strong search demand for therapy in the GTA, but a measurement problem: reported conversions were inflated by outbound button clicks and page views, while real calls went uncounted. The plan was to rebuild tracking around genuine enquiries first, then simplify the account to a focused set of psychotherapy Search campaigns so budget and data could concentrate on people ready to book.

  • Rebuild conversion tracking around real leads: dedicated call tracking (ads + website), lead-form submissions and JaneApp bookings.
  • Stop counting outbound button clicks and page views as conversions so bidding optimizes toward genuine enquiries.
  • Consolidate overlapping generic and Performance Max campaigns into focused psychotherapy Search campaigns.
  • Structure by intent — general psychotherapy, anxiety and trauma — with Maximize Conversions bidding on clean signals.

Gradual Improvement, Month by Month

The turnaround shows up clearly once tracking was fixed. After a January takeover month, February and March 2026 produced 36 and 40 genuine leads as call tracking came online and the focused Search campaigns took over. Through Q2 the account settled into a dependable 25–37 genuine leads a month at a conversion rate near 7.6% — several times the roughly 2% the account managed in 2025, when much of the reported activity was button clicks rather than real enquiries. Why the Tracking Rebuild Mattered

For most of 2025 this account's reported conversions were dominated by outbound button clicks and page views — actions that look like progress but do not put a client in front of a therapist. At their peak these vanity actions ran to well over a hundred a month. By rebuilding tracking around genuine phone calls, form submissions and JaneApp bookings, PPC Guru gave automated bidding something real to optimize toward. That single change is what lifted the conversion rate from about 2% to nearly 8% and let the practice see, for the first time, which searches actually produce enquiries.

Lessons for Therapy & Clinic Owners

  1. Track phone calls — for a therapy practice, calls are the highest-intent enquiry and often the majority.
  2. Do not let outbound button clicks or page views count as conversions; they inflate results and mislead bidding.
  3. Consolidate overlapping campaigns so budget and data concentrate where they can be optimized.
  4. Structure by intent — anxiety, trauma, couples — so ads match what each person is searching for.
  5. Fix measurement before scaling budget, so growth is built on real leads rather than vanity metrics.

Where They Stand Today

In its first full quarter under PPC Guru (April–June 2026) Therapy Villa generated about 31 genuine phone, form and booking enquiries a month at roughly $75 per lead, with a conversion rate near 7.6%. That is up from about 17 genuine leads a month in 2025 at a higher cost per lead — and because phone calls are now tracked for the first time, the practice finally has an honest view of which searches bring in clients. With clean measurement and a focused account, Therapy Villa has a dependable, scalable basis for continued growth.
