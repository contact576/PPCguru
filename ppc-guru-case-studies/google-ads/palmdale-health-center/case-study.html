PPC Guru launched a fresh Google Ads account for a Toronto physiotherapy and massage health centre in early 2026 and, in the first full quarter under management (April–June 2026), grew it to about nine genuine phone-call leads a month at roughly $59 each — up from a single tracked call in the launch month, with a conversion rate near 9%.

About the Client

Palmdale Health Center is a physiotherapy and registered massage therapy (RMT) practice serving patients across Toronto, including the Scarborough and North York areas. The clinic offers hands-on physiotherapy, massage therapy and related wellness treatments, and books patients through the JaneApp scheduling system. Like most local clinics, its growth depends on a steady flow of new-patient enquiries — phone calls and online bookings from people searching for treatment nearby — which makes reliable call tracking central to measuring what the advertising actually produces.

How the Account Came to PPC Guru

The clinic wanted a measurable, well-tracked Google Ads presence built around genuine patient enquiries rather than vanity clicks. PPC Guru launched a fresh Search account in early 2026 with call tracking and lead measurement in place from day one, then grew spend month by month as the phone-call data proved the channel was working.

The Challenge

Launching a new local health-centre account means starting from zero data. Early on, spend has to work hard for its first genuine enquiries, and the account's real conversion signals — phone calls to the clinic — need to be separated from Google Business Profile local actions and booking-page button clicks that can otherwise make raw conversion counts look larger than the true number of patient enquiries.

What We Analyzed & Planned

Because this was a launch rather than a turnaround, PPC Guru put measurement first: wire up call tracking and lead signals before scaling, focus the budget on high-intent local searches, and let genuine phone-call volume — not raw click counts — guide how quickly to grow.

  • Stand up call tracking (website calls and calls from ads) plus lead-form and JaneApp booking signals from launch.
  • Separate genuine phone-call leads from Google Business Profile local actions and booking-button clicks so reporting reflects real enquiries.
  • Concentrate budget on high-intent local searches such as 'physiotherapy near me' and 'massage therapy near me'.
  • Grow spend gradually, only as monthly phone-lead volume confirmed the channel was converting.

Why It Worked

  • Call tracking was in place from launch, so budget decisions followed genuine phone enquiries.
  • Two focused Search campaigns kept spend on core physiotherapy and massage searches.
  • Genuine-lead reporting kept vanity actions out of the numbers used to scale.
  • Budget grew only as the phone-lead data proved the channel was converting.

Lessons for Clinic Owners

  1. Track phone calls from the first day — for a local clinic, calls are the highest-intent enquiry.
  2. Separate genuine calls from Google Business Profile local actions and booking-button clicks so your reported numbers stay honest.
  3. Give a new account a few months to gather data before judging cost per lead.
  4. Scale budget gradually, following genuine lead volume rather than raw click counts.
  5. Keep negative keywords current so local searches reach the right neighbourhoods.

Where They Stand Today

In just its first full quarter under management, Palmdale Health Center went from a single tracked phone lead in the launch month to about nine genuine phone-call leads a month across April–June 2026, peaking at 12 in June, at roughly $59 per lead and a conversion rate near 9%. The account now has clean call tracking, a focused two-campaign structure and a clear, data-backed basis to keep scaling as demand grows.
