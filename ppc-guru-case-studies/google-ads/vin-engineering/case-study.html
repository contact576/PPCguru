About the Client

Vin Engineering INC is an engineering-services firm based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada, offering city-permit and engineering-drawing services to homeowners and contractors. Its enquiries are considered, higher-value requests — a call or a quote form from someone who needs stamped drawings or permit support for a project. Google Ads reaches those searchers at the point of need, so capturing each call and quote request accurately is central to the account from the very first month.

How the Account Came to PPC Guru

Vin Engineering partnered with PPC Guru in mid-2026 to launch its Google Ads presence. As a fresh build, the plan was a single, tightly focused Search campaign around a clear city-permit offer, paired with call and lead-form tracking so every genuine enquiry could be measured from day one.

The Challenge

As a brand-new account with a specialized, higher-value service, Vin Engineering had to build genuine demand from a standing start, with no history for bidding to learn from and lower monthly enquiry volumes than a high-frequency consumer service. That makes clean tracking of every call and quote request essential, so early budget decisions rest on real enquiries rather than raw clicks.

What We Analyzed & Planned

PPC Guru built the account around one focused Search campaign tied to a specific permit offer, and set up conversion tracking first: website and ad call tracking, plus lead-form conversions for the site's main and pop-up quote forms. With a clear offer and clean tracking, early budget could be judged on genuine enquiries rather than clicks.

  • Set up call tracking for both website calls and ad calls to the Toronto line.

Why It Worked

  • Call and quote-form tracking was in place from the first day of spend.
  • A single focused Search campaign kept budget on a clear permit offer.
  • Both online quote requests and phone calls were captured as genuine leads.
  • The first full month produced measurable enquiries, not just clicks.

Lessons for Professional-Services Firms

  1. Track calls and quote requests from day one — for higher-value services, every enquiry counts.
  2. Anchor a new campaign to one clear offer so budget and messaging stay focused.
  3. Expect a short learning ramp before a new account's first full month of volume.
  4. Judge early spend on genuine enquiries, not clicks.
  5. Give a specialized, lower-volume account a full quarter before drawing conclusions.

Where They Stand Today

Vin Engineering has an early but working Google Ads lead engine: after a short launch ramp in June 2026, its first full month (July 2026) delivered 12 genuine leads — eight online quote requests and four phone calls — at about $124 each from a single focused permit-offer campaign. This is a very early snapshot on a young account. PPC Guru's recommended next step is to let the account complete a full quarter and re-confirm the trend before scaling budget.
