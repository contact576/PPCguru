PPC Guru launched an immigration consultancy on Google Ads in September 2025 and, on a flat budget of around $760 a month, scaled it from 55 genuine leads in its first month to roughly 95 a month in the most recent quarter (Apr–Jun 2026) — at a cost per lead of about $8, driven mainly by high-intent phone calls.

About the Client

The client is an immigration consultancy based in Toronto, Canada, helping individuals and families with visa, residency and immigration applications. Its enquiries are overwhelmingly phone calls from people actively researching their options — a high-intent, time-sensitive service where speaking to a consultant quickly matters. Google Ads is the firm's primary channel for reaching those searchers at the moment they need help.

How the Account Came to PPC Guru

The consultancy came to PPC Guru to build its paid-search presence from scratch. The account launched in September 2025 with a modest budget of around $720 a month, combining a Search campaign for high-intent immigration queries with a Performance Max campaign to extend reach. The goal from the outset was straightforward: a steady, affordable flow of genuine enquiries — mostly phone calls — from people ready to talk.

The Challenge

Launching a brand-new account in a competitive, high-value category like immigration means starting with no conversion history and keeping cost per lead low enough for a modest budget to work hard. Early clicks needed to be converted into genuine phone calls efficiently, and the reporting had to stay honest — the account also records plenty of Google local actions and pop-up clicks that can flatter the numbers if they are counted as leads.

What We Analyzed & Planned

PPC Guru set the account up around genuine lead measurement — call tracking plus lead-form submissions — so that from launch the numbers reflected real enquiries rather than button taps or Business Profile actions. A Search campaign captured the highest-intent immigration searches, while Performance Max broadened reach; the ongoing work was to concentrate spend on what converted and let cost per lead fall as the data matured.

  • Launch a Search campaign for high-intent immigration queries plus a Performance Max campaign for reach.
  • Track genuine phone calls and lead-form submissions, separating them from Google local actions and pop-up clicks.
  • Use Maximize Conversions bidding and concentrate budget on the searches that produced real enquiries.
  • Grow lead volume on a flat budget by improving conversion rate and cost per lead.

Keeping the Lead Count Honest

Immigration accounts attract a lot of low-value signals — Google Maps directions, Business Profile taps and on-site pop-up clicks — that can make results look far bigger than they are. This account is measured on genuine phone calls and lead-form submissions only. That discipline is why the reported ~95 leads a month is a number the consultancy can trust: each one is a real enquiry, not a button click, and it keeps bidding optimising toward people who actually pick up the phone.

Lessons for Consultancy Owners

  1. A modest budget can produce strong volume in a high-intent category if cost per lead is kept low from day one.
  2. Count genuine calls and form submissions only — Maps directions and pop-up clicks flatter the numbers without adding enquiries.
  3. Pair a focused Search campaign with Performance Max to capture intent and extend reach.
  4. For time-sensitive services like immigration, phone calls are the highest-value action — optimise toward them.
  5. Growth doesn't have to mean a bigger budget; a better conversion rate does the same job.

Where They Stand Today

Less than a year after launch, the consultancy runs a dependable Google Ads program generating about 95 genuine leads a month — the large majority high-intent phone calls — at roughly $8 each in the most recent complete quarter (April–June 2026). That is up from 55 leads at about $13 each in its first month, on essentially the same budget. With honest call-and-form measurement in place, the firm has a scalable, affordable channel it can grow with confidence.
