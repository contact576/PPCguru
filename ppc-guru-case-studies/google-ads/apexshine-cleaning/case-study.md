## Launching a Cleaning Company to 38 Google Ads Leads a Month

**Headline results:** 38 Genuine leads · ~$50 Cost per lead · +27% More leads vs first month

PPC Guru launched Apexshine Cleaning on Google Ads across Local Services and Search, and in just its second full month the account produced 38 genuine phone and form leads at about $50 each — a 27% increase over its first month, with cost per lead down roughly a quarter.

### About the Client
Apexshine Cleaning Inc. is a cleaning-services company serving commercial and residential clients across the Greater Toronto Area in Ontario, Canada. The business relies on a steady stream of enquiries — phone calls and quote requests from people and offices that need regular or one-off cleaning. Google Ads reaches those buyers at the moment they search, and because cleaning enquiries come heavily by phone, accurate call tracking is central to the account.

### How the Account Came to PPC Guru
Apexshine Cleaning partnered with PPC Guru in mid-2026 to build its Google Ads presence from scratch. The plan was a two-front launch: Google Local Services Ads to capture ready-to-book local demand, plus dedicated Search campaigns split by commercial and residential intent — all measured with proper call and form tracking.

### The Challenge
As a brand-new account, Apexshine had to build genuine demand across two very different buyer types — commercial and residential — from a standing start, with no history for bidding to learn from. Cleaning enquiries are also phone-heavy, so capturing calls accurately (and keeping map actions out of the lead count) was essential to steer budget toward real work.

### What We Analyzed & Planned
PPC Guru set up call tracking (ad calls and website calls to the Toronto line) and a lead-form conversion, then separated genuine calls and forms from Google Business Profile map actions so reporting would reflect real enquiries. The account was structured into three parts — Local Services, a Commercial Search campaign and a Residential Search campaign — so budget and messaging could match each type of buyer.

- Launch Google Local Services Ads to capture ready-to-book local cleaning demand.
- Build separate Commercial and Residential Search campaigns to match distinct buyer intent.
- Set up call tracking for ad calls and website calls, plus a lead-form conversion.
- Exclude Google Business Profile local actions from the lead count so reporting stays honest.

### Why a Local Services + Search Combination Works for Cleaning
Cleaning buyers split into two very different groups — offices booking recurring commercial contracts and homeowners booking one-off or regular cleans. Running Google Local Services Ads alongside dedicated Commercial and Residential Search campaigns lets the account meet both: Local Services captures pay-per-lead calls from ready-to-book customers, while the two Search campaigns tailor messaging and budget to each intent. Tracking real calls and forms across all three — and excluding map actions — is what keeps the lead count honest and points bidding at genuine bookings.

### Lessons for Home-Services Owners
1. Track phone calls as conversions — cleaning enquiries come mostly by phone.
2. Split commercial and residential intent so budget and messaging match the buyer.
3. Use Local Services Ads to capture ready-to-book, pay-per-lead demand.
4. Keep Google Business Profile map actions out of the lead count for honest reporting.
5. Give a new account a few months before drawing firm conclusions.
