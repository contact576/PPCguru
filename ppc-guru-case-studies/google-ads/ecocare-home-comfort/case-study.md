## How an HVAC Company Grew to ~14 Google Ads Leads a Month in Two Quarters

**Headline results:** ~14 Genuine leads / month · +35% More leads vs prior quarter · ~7 in 10 Leads by phone call

PPC Guru launched EcoCare Home Comfort on Google Ads with call and form tracking wired in from day one, then grew it from about 10 genuine leads a month in its first full quarter to roughly 14 a month in the most recent quarter (Apr–Jun 2026) — a 35% quarter-over-quarter increase, with about seven in ten leads arriving as direct phone calls.

### About the Client
EcoCare Home Comfort is a home-comfort and HVAC company serving homeowners across Ontario, Canada. The business installs and services heating, cooling and air-conditioning systems, and its growth depends on a steady flow of homeowner enquiries — phone calls and quote requests from people who need a repair, a replacement or a new system installed. Google Ads is the company's channel for reaching those high-intent searchers at the exact moment they are looking for help, which makes reliable call and form tracking central to the account.

### How the Account Came to PPC Guru
EcoCare Home Comfort brought its home-comfort advertising to PPC Guru in late 2025 for a fresh, measurement-first Google Ads build. Rather than chase clicks, the goal was a lead engine the business could trust — one where every phone call and quote request is tracked, so budget could be scaled on proven demand rather than guesswork.

### The Challenge
As a fresh Google Ads build, the account had to prove the channel from a standing start — establishing trustworthy tracking, learning which homeowner searches turn into booked work, and building enough conversion volume for automated bidding to optimize against. HVAC demand is also seasonal and phone-heavy, so measuring calls accurately was essential before any budget could be scaled with confidence.

### What We Analyzed & Planned
Before spending began, PPC Guru set up the measurement first: call tracking on ads and the website, a lead-form conversion, and an offline import path for booked jobs. A Performance Max campaign was then launched to reach homeowners across Search, Maps and Google's wider inventory, with automated bidding pointed at genuine leads rather than surface-level clicks.

### Why Call Tracking Comes First in HVAC
Homeowners with a broken furnace or air conditioner call — they rarely fill in a form and wait. That is why the account was built around call tracking from day one, capturing both clicks-to-call on ads and calls placed from the website's toll-free line. About seven in ten genuine leads in the most recent quarter arrived by phone. Tracking those calls as conversions — rather than counting button clicks or map views — is what lets automated bidding find more of the searches that turn into booked service calls, and gives the business an honest line of sight from ad spend to real enquiries.

### Lessons for Home-Services Owners
1. Track phone calls as conversions — in home services, the phone is the primary lead, not the form.
2. Set up tracking before you scale spend, so growth is based on real enquiries.
3. Give a new account a full quarter to gather data before judging automated bidding.
4. Plan budget around season — HVAC demand swings, so scale into the peaks.
5. Feed booked and completed jobs back to Google so bidding optimizes toward real work.
