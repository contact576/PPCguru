About the Client

Rehab2Go is a mobile physiotherapy and rehabilitation service that brings treatment to patients in their own homes across Toronto. Its patients often search specifically for at-home care — in-home physiotherapy, home-visit rehab, and physiotherapy for seniors or post-surgery recovery. Because the service is delivered at home rather than at a clinic, phone calls and form enquiries are the core way new patients get in touch, which makes reliable lead tracking essential from the outset.

How the Account Came to PPC Guru

Rehab2Go came to PPC Guru to build a Google Ads presence from scratch and reach people actively searching for at-home physiotherapy. PPC Guru launched the account in early 2026 with call and form tracking in place, starting on Search and later adding Performance Max to widen reach.

The Challenge

Starting a brand-new mobile-physiotherapy account means building demand and data at the same time. At-home physiotherapy is a specific, lower-volume search category, so the early goal was a clean tracking foundation and relevant traffic rather than large lead numbers — and then a way to widen reach without losing that relevance.

What We Analyzed & Planned

As a brand-new account, the priority was a clean measurement foundation and tightly relevant early traffic. PPC Guru set up genuine-lead tracking first, focused Search on high-intent at-home queries, and then introduced Performance Max to expand reach once the tracking was trusted.

  • Set up genuine-lead tracking (phone calls and form submissions) before scaling spend.
  • Focus Search on high-intent at-home queries such as 'in home physiotherapy toronto' and 'mobile physiotherapist'.
  • Add a Performance Max campaign to widen reach beyond core Search once tracking was trusted.
  • Keep early traffic relevant with ongoing search-term and negative-keyword work.

Gradual Improvement, Month by Month

Across its first five months the account produced 7, 4, 5, 7 and 9 genuine leads a month — a low but steady flow while the campaigns gathered data. The clearest shift so far is in reach: after Performance Max was added, monthly impressions grew from a few hundred to about 18,000 by July, with clicks rising from 67 to 275. Lead count is growing more gradually, which is expected for a specialised at-home service in its first months.

Why It Worked

  • A clean tracking foundation meant every reported lead was a genuine phone or form enquiry.
  • Early Search budget stayed on high-intent at-home queries.
  • Performance Max expanded reach once the account had trustworthy conversion signals.
  • Ongoing search-term work kept a small budget relevant.

Lessons for At-Home Care Providers

  1. Put genuine-lead tracking in place before scaling — phone calls and forms are the enquiries that matter.
  2. Expect specialised, at-home search categories to start at lower volume than clinic-based ones.
  3. Prove relevance on Search before widening reach with Performance Max.
  4. Give a new account several months of data before judging cost per lead.
  5. Watch reach and lead count together, so growth in impressions translates into real enquiries.

Where They Stand Today

Rehab2Go is early in its Google Ads journey. In its first five months the account has built a clean tracking foundation and a steady early flow of four to nine genuine phone and form leads a month, and reach has expanded roughly 38-fold since launch after Performance Max was introduced. The next phase is about converting that widened reach into a higher, efficient lead volume — the account is a work in progress with the fundamentals in place, and is flagged for continued review as more data accumulates.
