About the Client

The client is a CAD drafting and engineering-drawing firm serving the Greater Toronto Area, producing technical drawings and city-permit drawing packages for renovation and construction projects. Its enquiries come from property owners, contractors and builders searching for drafting and permit help — high-intent phone calls and form submissions. Google Ads is the firm's primary channel for capturing that demand at the moment people are looking for a drafter.

How the Account Came to PPC Guru

The firm partnered with PPC Guru to build its Google Ads presence from scratch in January 2023. Rather than starting small and ramping, the account launched at a full working budget of around $2,900 a month, with the goal of turning search demand for drafting and permit drawings into a predictable flow of enquiries. From day one the brief was clear: measurable calls and form leads, not just clicks.

The Challenge

Building a new account for a specialised service is a cold start: no historical conversion data, and drafting and permit searches sit alongside plenty of loosely related queries. Early on the campaigns drew heavy traffic — several thousand clicks a month — at a cost per lead north of $160. The challenge was to convert that raw traffic into qualified drafting and permit enquiries and drive the cost per lead down, all without increasing the monthly budget.

What We Analyzed & Planned

PPC Guru built the account around the two highest-value demand pockets — general drafting services and city-permit drawings for the GTA — and wired up genuine call and lead-form tracking before optimising. Early months deliberately cast a wide net to gather search-term data; the work from there was disciplined refinement: cutting irrelevant queries, concentrating budget on converting searches, and letting Maximize Conversions bidding compound the gains.

  • Launch dedicated Search campaigns for drafting services and city-permit drawings (GTA).
  • Track genuine phone calls and lead-form submissions, including a dedicated permit-offer landing page.
  • Mine early search-term data and build out negatives to cut wasted clicks.
  • Run Maximize Conversions bidding and let cost per lead fall as the data matured — without raising budget.

Why a Flat Budget Tells the Real Story

It is easy to grow leads by spending more. This account did the opposite: monthly budget barely moved from launch to today, yet leads roughly tripled and cost per lead fell by about two-thirds. That gap is the value of the management itself — every additional lead came from better keyword targeting, tighter negatives and smarter bidding, not a bigger cheque. For a specialised drafting and permit business, that efficiency is what makes the channel sustainable.

Lessons for Service-Business Owners

  1. A new account is a data-gathering exercise first — expect a higher cost per lead in month one and judge it on the trend.
  2. Separate distinct services (general drafting vs permit drawings) so each gets the right keywords and budget.
  3. Negatives are where the savings live — prune irrelevant searches relentlessly in the early months.
  4. Track genuine calls and form submissions, not button clicks, so bidding optimises toward real enquiries.
  5. More leads don't require more budget — efficiency on a flat spend is often the bigger win.

Where They Stand Today

In the most recent complete quarter (April–June 2026) the account generated about 55 genuine call and form leads a month at roughly $51 each — up from 18 leads at about $162 each in its launch month, on essentially the same budget. With cost per lead cut by about two-thirds through management alone, the firm has a dependable, efficient channel it can rely on.
